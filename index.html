Paper Shapes

Little stories you play with your hands.

Paper Shapes is a set of short, interactive stories for young children, starring four friends: Circle, Square, Triangle and Cross. Each story is told in a few steps, and the child acts on each one by tapping, tilting the device, blowing, clapping and so on.

The stories are about small, everyday things: sharing, patience, worrying less, listening, trying again. There is also a story that introduces each friend, a guessing game about sides and corners, a bedtime story and a game of football. Stories can be read aloud by your device's voice.

You can make your own activities too, and share them with other people. A Home Screen widget keeps the friends one tap away.

Everything stays on your device. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking.
